## Full description (both stores)

> Scoreboard is a live scorekeeper for racquet sports. Score the match from your phone at the side of the court, and the score shows up live on your stream, on a TV at the venue, or on anyone's phone.
>
> SIX SPORTS, REAL RULES
> Badminton, tennis, padel, pickleball, table tennis and squash, each with its official rules plus the shorter formats clubs actually play: BWF 21 and 15-point, tennis with tiebreaks, no-ad and Fast4, padel golden point and star point, pickleball side-out and rally scoring, table tennis 11 and 21, squash PAR and hand-in hand-out. Singles and doubles.
>
> SCORING THAT KEEPS UP
> Tap the side that won the rally. Scoreboard tracks who serves and from which court, switches ends, and calls game point, set point and match point for you. Made a mistake? Undo as many points as you need, or correct the score directly.
>
> SHOW IT LIVE
> Every match gets its own links. Add the overlay to OBS, Streamlabs or StreamYard as a browser source for a broadcast-style score on your stream, or open the full-screen scoreboard on a TV or tablet for players and spectators. Share a QR code so anyone can follow along. Pick from a range of overlay and scoreboard themes.
>
> BUILT FOR THE VENUE
> Every point is saved on your phone first, so patchy venue Wi-Fi never loses a point; it syncs as soon as you're back online. The screen stays awake while you score.
>
> THE WHOLE MATCH, NOT JUST THE SCORE
> Add players, court, round and category. Record time-outs, walkovers, retirements and suspensions. Your last format and recent player names are remembered, so the next match takes seconds to set up.
>
> FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
> No sign-up needed to start scoring. Sign in only if you want your matches kept with your account across devices. Scoreboard is open source under the MIT licence.
